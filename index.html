SNRSTAKING V7 CANONS

Built around transparent, programmable on-chain rules verified directly on BscScan.

CANON 01

True Auto-Compound 45%

What it does: Reinvests 45% of yield back into principal participation balance.

Why it exists: Accelerates long-term position compounding while reducing selling pressure.

CANON 02

Weighted Average Lock Time

What it does: Mathematically averages lock remaining time when top-ups occur.

Why it exists: Prevents small top-ups from resetting large stakes back to full duration.

CANON 03

Direct Active Qualification

What it does: Qualifies downlines with min 100 SNR stake for Direct Active metrics.

Why it exists: Lowers entry barriers while ensuring real capital commitment.

CANON 04

Dynamic Referral Compression

What it does: Traverses up to 50 levels up to find 10 qualified uplines.

Why it exists: Guarantees active working leaders receive network incentives.

CANON 05

Anti-Fragile Unstake Penalty

What it does: Enforces 50% penalty on early unstakes (25% Treasury, 25% Reserve).

Why it exists: Disincentivizes panic exits and enriches liquidity reserve.

CANON 06

300% Max Reward & Leader Cap

What it does: Caps total rewards at 300% and leader rewards at 100% of stake.

Why it exists: Prevents hyper-inflation and obligates leaders to re-inject capital.
